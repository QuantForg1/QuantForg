# 2. Differentiation strategy

QuantForg should not claim to be the world’s best trading platform. The evidence on 19 November 2026 supports a narrower position.

## Position

**QuantForg is MT5 software for XAUUSD that explains what the engine decided, checks risk before an order, and leaves the money at the customer’s broker.**

That sentence is already implied by the production homepage. The growth program’s job is to make it easier to find, easier to verify, and harder to confuse with a signal service or a profit promise.

## Gaps QuantForg can realistically own

These are gaps in the competitor set above, matched to capabilities that already exist in this repository (`decision_evidence`, `paper`, `execution_intelligence`, `risk`, `broker_connectivity`). Owning them means publishing and showing records. It does not mean new trading logic.

1. **Decision records, not signals.** Competitors sell a BUY or a score. QuantForg can show the actual decision (buy, sell, wait, hold, reject), the instrument, the time, the conditions that passed, the conditions that failed, the risk check, and whether an order was authorized. If a field is absent, the UI says absent. No synthesized “AI reasoning.”

2. **Analysis is not an order.** The homepage already says this. Educational pages and the assistant should repeat the boundary in plain language: a chart reading, a shadow outcome, and a live fill are three different objects and must not share one P/L number.

3. **Ceilings the customer cannot loosen.** Grid, martingale, and averaging-down stay disabled. A plan does not bypass a risk check. This is unusual in EA marketing and should be explained as a limitation that protects the account, not as a performance edge.

4. **Broker quality without a league table.** Measure connection, spread, slippage, rejects, and freshness per connected account when the data exists. Do not rank Weltrade against IC Markets until the samples are comparable. Most affiliate “best gold broker” pages fail this test. QuantForg can win trust by refusing the ranking.

5. **A learning record that does not retune live trading.** Compare what the engine expected with what the broker recorded. Proposed parameter changes stay in research until a person authorizes a release. That is the opposite of an auto-optimizing robot.

6. **A public site that matches the product.** Eight indexable URLs cannot carry this position. The missing pages are explanations of the product that already exists, not new strategies.

## Gaps QuantForg should not pretend to own

| Tempting claim | Why it is not available |
| --- | --- |
| Broad multi-asset AI terminal | Public trading universe is XAUUSD. TradingView and QuantConnect cover far more markets |
| Free always-on robot | The robot requires a paid plan. A free connect-and-verify path exists; calling that a free robot would be false |
| Audited track record or AUM | The site correctly refuses sample performance. None was found to publish |
| Licensed broker or Google-certified forex advertiser | No license or certification is claimed on the homepage, and none was verified in this research |
| Cheapest AI tool | Composer and TradingView publish lower entry prices. Price is not the wedge |
| “Institutions use us” | The word institutional describes the software style. It is not a client list |

## Audience

**Primary.** A person who already has, or is willing to open, an MT5 account at a supported broker, cares about gold, and will pay $199–$799 per month for software that can say no.

**Secondary.** A researcher who wants to read decisions and shadow outcomes before allowing live sends.

**Not the target of the first content program.** US equity day traders (Trade Ideas), multi-asset quants who want to write LEAN code (QuantConnect), and shoppers looking for a martingale gold EA.

## Message pillars

Use only language the product can demonstrate.

| Pillar | Say | Do not say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Control of funds | Your broker holds the account. QuantForg does not custody cash | “We manage your capital” |
| Evidence | Every published decision ties to a stored record | Invented confidence scores or narrative |
| Risk | A failed check means no new order. Stops are required. Gaps can still lose more than the plan | “Risk-free”, “protected profits”, “cannot lose” |
| Silence | No setup means no trade | “Trades every day” or “always in the market” |
| Software | A subscription is access to software | “Expected return”, “proven ROI” |

## How this shows up in product, without touching the engine

- Public docs that point at the decision, paper, and execution screens a signed-in user already has.
- Owner growth views that count real registrations, invoices, and content performance once those sources are connected.
- A review gate before any page states a financial fact, a broker fact, or a regulatory fact.

The trading core stays frozen. Differentiation is presentation, measurement, and editorial discipline around evidence that already exists.
