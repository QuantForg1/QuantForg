# 9. Marketing channel strategy

## Operating rules

- One landing page per campaign. The page must answer what the product does not do.
- UTM or another parameter is stored as a first-touch attribute on the account only if the privacy notice allows it.
- Cost is blank when there is no invoice. It is not estimated.
- Creative that shows a winning trade is not allowed.
- Community posts are written by a person. Automation may suggest a draft. It does not post.

## What “working” means

A channel is kept when, over a stated period, the billing service shows finished invoices whose first touch is that channel, and support is not dominated by people who misunderstood the product as a profit service. Until both systems are queried, the channel is “not measured.”
