Frequently Asked Questions

How does QuantForg work?

You create an account, connect your own broker, and grant permissions. Analysis can show a research signal. An order is sent only after the existing risk checks pass. A broker fill is separate from the signal.

Is my account safe?

Your capital stays at your broker. QuantForg does not display broker passwords after entry. Risk checks and broker permissions can still block an order.

What symbols can I trade?

The live desk is XAUUSD (Gold). The signed-in Markets screen shows what the broker session actually returns.

How are the results calculated?

Completed results come from broker deals after you connect. This page does not publish sample win rates or profit figures.

Which brokers are supported?

The production connection is Weltrade through MetaTrader 5. You connect the account you own.

What happens after I connect a broker?

The session loads status, quotes, and positions from that login. Execution still waits for risk validation. Open positions stay under the existing stop rules.

Can I use copy trading?

QuantForg does not offer a separate copy-trading product. Automatic execution, when enabled, runs on your connected account.
